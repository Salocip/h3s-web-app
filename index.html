H3S Web App

Minimal BLE control for QCY earbuds and headsets. Protocols recovered from QCY 4.0.7. Chromium or Chrome required.

Web Bluetooth is disabled in this browser. On Linux, launch Chromium with --enable-features=WebBluetooth (see run.sh).
